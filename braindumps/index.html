What are braindumps?

Whenever I'm bored and have nothing to do but think and write, or when I have a particularly entertaining thought that I don't want to forget, I write them down. I've named them braindumps. The following are a selection of those deemed fit for public view.

As for why I'm publishing them for anyone to read, I guess I'm offering them as an entry point to getting to know me. To get to know what I usually ponder about, and what kinds of thoughts and opinions I have on whatever topics. That plus I just have a habit of oversharing. (Maybe I'm narcissistic?)

This page is manually updated from time to time. Some braindumps may not be in English.
