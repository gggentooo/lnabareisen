What are braindumps?

Whenever I'm bored and have nothing to do but think and write, or when I have a particularly entertaining thought that I don't want to forget, I write them down. I've named them braindumps. The following are a selection of those deemed fit for public view.

As for why I'm publishing them for anyone to read, I guess I'm offering them as an entry point to getting to know me. To get to know what I usually ponder about, and what kinds of thoughts and opinions I have on whatever topics.

This page is manually updated from time to time. Some braindumps may not be in English.

As in, I usually can’t make the reason behind needing that new rule to go away in the first place. So, regardless of if I make a new rule for myself or not, I still have a problem to tackle. Which means I always have a thing to mess up, and the rule is there to help me not mess it up.
